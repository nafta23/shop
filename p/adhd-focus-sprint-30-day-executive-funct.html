Traditional productivity advice tells you to try harder. For an ADHD brain, trying harder is like flooring the gas pedal while your parking brake is locked. You don't need more willpower; you need less friction and a clear map of how your energy actually works. Over the next five days, we aren't building complex habit loops. We are dismantling the invisible barriers that drain your executive function before you even open your laptop. ADHD energy doesn't follow a neat 9-to-5 linear curve. It operates on interest, novelty, and urgency. For the next 48 hours, stop fighting your natural rhythms and start tracking them. Carry a physical notebook or use a blank document to log your energy and focus levels every two hours on a scale of 1 to 5. Note what you were doing right before a spike or a crash. Did a random text message derail a two-hour flow state? Did a high-stakes deadline create hyperfocus, but leave you completely paralyzed by 3:00 PM?
